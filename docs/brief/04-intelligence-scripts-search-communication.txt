# MASTER PROMPT — AI INTELLIGENCE LAYER + SCRIPTS + SEARCH + COMMUNICATION

**IMPORTANT: THIS IS AN ADDITIVE EXTENSION TO THE EXISTING MASTER PROMPT.**

Do not remove, rewrite, simplify, replace, or weaken anything already defined in the existing master prompt.

Everything already specified remains valid.

This section adds a deeper **AI Intelligence Layer, Universal Intelligence Search, Source Finding, Account Memory, Scripts, Communication, WhatsApp capabilities, and contextual AI actions** across the workspace.

---

# 1. THE AI INTELLIGENCE LAYER

The workspace should not feel like a normal dashboard with a chatbot bolted onto it.

AI should function as an **intelligence layer across the entire Brand OS**.

The system should continuously understand the information already present across:

* Workspace records
* Brand Guidelines
* Research
* Strategy
* Campaigns
* Content Calendar
* Creative & Production
* Scripts
* Assets
* Scope
* Decisions
* Files
* Meetings
* Tasks / Actions
* Team
* Google Drive
* Google Sheets
* Excel
* Gmail
* Google Calendar
* Connected Canva sources
* Future external systems
* WhatsApp / communication systems where connected

The fundamental model is:

**DOCUMENT → UNDERSTAND → EXTRACT → CLASSIFY → CONNECT → PROPOSE → CONFIRM → UPDATE**

The AI should understand what something means before deciding where it belongs.

The workspace database remains the source of truth.

The user remains the final authority.

AI can identify, suggest, connect, summarise, search, draft and propose changes, but meaningful changes to the workspace should normally require confirmation unless the user has explicitly enabled an automation.

Never fabricate an action, approval, integration, message, upload, sync or external update that did not actually happen.

# 2. UNIVERSAL AI UPLOAD

There should be a universal **Upload / Add to Intelligence** capability available from Pulse and relevant sections of the workspace.

Users should be able to upload:

* Meeting minutes
* Meeting transcripts
* PDFs
* Word documents
* Presentations
* Briefs
* Research documents
* Strategy documents
* Content documents
* Scripts
* Shoot briefs
* Client feedback
* Reference documents
* Spreadsheets
* Other relevant account files

The AI should understand the document and identify what information is relevant.

The upload should be **page-aware**.

For example:

Uploading a document from:

**Strategy**

should cause the AI to primarily look for strategic information.

Uploading from:

**Creative & Production**

should cause it to look for production information.

Uploading from:

**Content Calendar**

should cause it to look for content-related information.

Uploading from:

**Pulse**

should treat it as potentially account-wide information.

Also provide an optional instruction field:

**“Tell AI what to look for”**

For example:

> “Find anything related to the September campaign.”

or

> “Pull out all client approvals.”

or

> “Find the new deliverables mentioned here.”

# 3. MEETING INTELLIGENCE

When a meeting transcript, recording transcript, minutes document or notes are uploaded, AI should distinguish between:

* Discussion
* Ideas
* Questions
* Decisions
* Approvals
* Rejected ideas
* Unresolved items
* Actions
* Changes
* Deadlines
* Dependencies
* Client responsibilities
* Agency responsibilities

Do not treat every sentence as a decision.

Do not treat discussion as approval.

Do not infer approval where approval was not explicitly given or sufficiently evidenced.

After processing, show:

**Found X things**

with grouped sections:

### Decisions

### Approvals

### Actions

### Updates

### Unresolved

Each extracted item should be individually:

* Approved
* Rejected
* Edited
* Assigned
* Linked
* Added to workspace

Also allow:

**Approve all**

where appropriate.

# 11. SEARCH ACROSS THE ENTIRE ACCOUNT GRAPH

The AI Search should be able to search across:

**Workspace + Files + Gmail + Drive + Calendar + Canva + Sheets + Meetings + Decisions + Content + Production + Assets + Strategy + Campaigns + Scripts + Scope**

where the relevant integrations and permissions exist.

This should allow questions that require joining information across multiple sources.

For example:

> “What did we decide about the furniture campaign in the last meeting, and where is the latest visual reference?”

The AI should potentially:

1. Find the relevant meeting.
2. Identify the decision.
3. Find the relevant campaign.
4. Find the latest visual reference.
5. Verify its source/date.
6. Return the answer.
7. Show the relevant source records.

---

# 12. SOURCE-FIRST ANSWERS

One of the most important principles of AI Search:

**Do not only give the answer. Show where the answer came from.**

Every answer should, where possible, provide:

* Answer
* Source
* Date
* Person
* Relevant file/email/message
* Link to source
* Related workspace record

For example:

> **The latest product images were uploaded by the designer on 17 September.**

Then:

**Source**
Designer Upload — Google Drive
17 Sep 2026

**Related**
Furniture Campaign → Creative & Production → Assets

This makes the AI trustworthy.

# 13. PRECISION SEARCH

The AI should understand:

### WHO

> “What did Sarah upload?”

### WHAT

> “What did we write about the dining table?”

### WHEN

> “What happened around 15 September?”

### WHERE

> “Where is the original research?”

### WHY

> “Why did we choose this campaign idea?”

### WHO + WHAT + WHEN

> “What did the designer upload on Tuesday?”

### WHAT + SOURCE

> “Where did this insight come from?”

### WHAT + PERSON + ACCOUNT

> “What did the client approve last week?”

The system should interpret conversational language, aliases, shorthand, approximate dates and contextual references where possible.

If there are multiple possible matches, ask a concise clarification rather than guessing.

# 14. SEARCH RESULT DESIGN

Search results should not look like Google search results.

They should feel like **intelligence returning context**.

A response can contain:

### ANSWER

A concise human answer.

### WHY / CONTEXT

### SOURCES

The actual files, emails, meetings, records or external sources that support the answer.

### RELATED

Connected campaigns, content, people, assets, decisions or production records.

### ACTION

Where useful:

* Open
* View source
* Add to workspace
* Link
* Share
* Create task
* Create brief
* Send
* Add to calendar

The UI should stay calm and minimal.

# 32. WHATSAPP — QUICK SHARE

Examples:

> “Send this asset to the client.”

> “Open WhatsApp and send this for approval.”

> “Share the shoot details on WhatsApp.”

---

# 33. WHATSAPP — CONNECTED SENDING

Also architect the system for a deeper version:

**Connected WhatsApp**

This should support actual sending from the Brand OS where a legitimate WhatsApp Business Platform/API integration is connected and authorised.

The architecture should be:

**User request
→ AI understands intent
→ Finds recipient
→ Finds relevant workspace context
→ Finds asset / document / link
→ Drafts message
→ User confirms
→ WhatsApp integration sends
→ Delivery / message status is recorded where supported**

Do not imply that ChatGPT itself automatically gives the website WhatsApp sending capability.

A real WhatsApp Business Platform/API integration, appropriate business account setup, permissions and applicable platform messaging rules are required.

Never fake a connected WhatsApp integration.

# 36. AI FIND + COMMUNICATE

> “Find the latest version of the furniture shoot deck and send it to the client on WhatsApp.”

1. Find the latest relevant deck.
2. Verify its version/date.
3. Identify the intended recipient.

5. Show the user what will be sent.

The ultimate experience should be:

**I give the Brand OS information.**

↓

**It understands what it means.**

↓

**It knows where it belongs.**

↓

**It remembers it.**

↓

**It connects it to everything else.**

↓

**I can ask the workspace anything about the account.**

↓

**It finds the answer, even if that answer lives across Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Canva, meetings, content, strategy or files.**

↓

**It shows me the source.**

↓

**It can propose what should happen next.**

↓

**I confirm.**

↓

**The workspace updates.**

↓

**And when appropriate, it can communicate that information through email, WhatsApp or another connected channel.**

The goal is not to build a dashboard that happens to have AI.

The goal is to build a **living Brand OS with an intelligence layer underneath it.**

The user should feel:

> **“This thing actually knows the account.”**

And the most important principle:

**Minimal interface.
Maximum intelligence.
Everything connected.
Nothing lost.
Everything traceable.
The human remains in control.**
